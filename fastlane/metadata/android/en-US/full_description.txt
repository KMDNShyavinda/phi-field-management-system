PHI Smart Inspector is a 100% offline-first field management and health inspection application tailored for Public Health Inspectors (PHI) in Sri Lanka.

Designed to eliminate tedious paperwork and function without an internet connection or central server, PHI Smart Inspector empowers field officers to execute statutory health duties directly on their Android devices.

Key Features:
• Establishment Directory & Field Inspections: Register food businesses, bakeries, pharmacies, and hotels with GPS coordinates and QR codes. Conduct Food Act compliance checklists with risk ratings.
• Smart Legal Guide: Built-in legal reference with Food Act provisions, offense breakdowns, required court evidence, and magistrate prosecution steps (B-reports).
• Digital Signatures: Dual touchscreen signatures (PHI and business owner) embedded directly into tamper-proof inspection reports.
• Dengue Tracking Map: Real-time case logging with interactive OpenStreetMap GPS pin-pointing and dynamic 100-meter high-risk buffer zones.
• Automated Monthly Performance Reports (PDF): One-click generation of standard Ministry of Health monthly inspection reports ready for printing or instant sharing.
• Food Handlers' Health Certification: Track medical fitness certificates with automatic 30-day expiry notifications and 1-year renewal actions.
• Water & Food Samples Tracking: Log sampling seals, Government Analyst/MRI dispatch tracking, and official laboratory report outcomes.
• Schools & MCH Clinics Inspection: Monitor healthy school canteen circular compliance, vaccine cold-chain refrigeration temperatures (2°C - 8°C), and biomedical waste disposal.
• Google Drive Backup: Daily one-tap compressed backup of the local SQLite database and photo evidence to personal Google Drive.
• In-App Auto-Updater: Built-in update detector via GitHub Releases for seamless single-tap APK updates.

No external servers or recurring subscriptions are required. All records are stored securely on the local device using SQLite.
